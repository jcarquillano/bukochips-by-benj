Our Story

Buko Chips by Benj started with one goal—to create a healthier and more delicious coconut snack while proving that age is never a barrier to building a business.

Every pack is made from real coconuts that are carefully prepared, flavored, and baked to achieve a light, crispy texture without sacrificing the natural taste of coconut.

What began as a small student business has grown into a brand trusted by thousands of customers, partnered with local stores, and supported by resellers throughout the country.

Today, Buko Chips by Benj continues to innovate with new flavors, better production methods, and opportunities for aspiring entrepreneurs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do the chips last?
Our Buko Chips are best enjoyed fresh. Store them in a cool, dry place and keep the package tightly resealed after opening to maintain their crisp texture and delicious flavor. For the best experience, consume them before the expiration date printed on the packaging.
Do you ship nationwide?
Yes! We ship anywhere in the Philippines through trusted courier partners. Your order will be carefully packed to ensure it arrives fresh and ready to enjoy.
Do you offer reseller packages?
Absolutely! We welcome individuals, students, and entrepreneurs who want to start their own snack business. Our reseller package includes:

• Wholesale pricing
• Marketing materials
• Product selling guidance
• Dedicated promotional support
• Ongoing assistance for resellers
What flavors are available?
We currently offer the following delicious flavors:

• Original Buko Chips
• White Chocolate Buko Chips
• Dark Chocolate Buko Chips
• Matcha Buko Chips
• Strawberry Buko Chips
• Ube Buko Chips
• Buko Thins
Can I become a distributor?
Yes! We're continuously expanding our distribution network across the Philippines. If you're interested in becoming a distributor or reseller, we'd love to work with you. Contact us through our Facebook, Instagram, email, or phone number, and our team will guide you through the application process.
